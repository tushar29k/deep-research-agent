# ADR 0003 — extractive claims keep everything checkable

## Decision

Research claims are extracted sentences from fetched sources, never
summarised or reworded. The writer stitches, it doesn't paraphrase.

## Why

Rewording is where citations get fuzzy: the claim drifts from the
source and the critic's token-containment check becomes a judgement
call. Extracted sentences keep verifiability mechanical (a number or a
word is either in the source or it isn't), which is what makes the
100% verifiability number a real number instead of a vibe. Summarised
claims can come later behind the same critic; the seam is where claims
are made.

## Cost

Reports read like a well-filed dossier, not like an essay — choppy
prose in exchange for checkable prose. Accepted for v1.
