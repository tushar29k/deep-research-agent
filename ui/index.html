deep-research-agent

planner → researcher → critic → writer

A multi-agent research pipeline with one hard rule: no claim reaches the report without a citation the critic verified. Watch the pipeline stages fire, read the rejected fabrications, and check every citation against its source.

HOW IT WORKS

1 · Plan

The topic splits into three aspects — what it is, the numbers, the trade-offs — so research covers depth, not just surface.

2 · Research

Mock MCP web tools fetch candidate claims. Deliberately, a few fabricated claims are mixed in — real agents hallucinate mid-research.

3 · Critic

Every claim is token-checked against its cited source. Below 0.75 containment it's rejected; too many rejections triggers a wider re-run.

4 · Write

The writer only ever sees accepted claims, so the report is cited by construction. Rejected fabrications are shown below.
